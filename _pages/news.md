### July 2026 — iCARE Lab Website Launched

<div class="news-entry" markdown="1">

### July 2026 — Manuscript Submitted on 3D CT Angiography Enhancement

Our manuscript, **“Body-Aware 3D Wavelet Enhancement for Structure-Preserving CT Angiography Analysis,”** was submitted to *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control*. The work investigates structure-preserving enhancement of three-dimensional CT angiography data and evaluates its impact across multiple imaging datasets and downstream segmentation experiments.

</div>

<div class="news-entry" markdown="1">

### July 2026 — iCARE Lab Project Selected for KSU First-Year Scholars Program

My project, **“Exploring AI in Healthcare: 3D Medical Image Segmentation and Analysis,”** was selected for the **2026–2027 Kennesaw State University First-Year Scholars Program**.

The project will introduce first-year undergraduate students to artificial intelligence for healthcare, with a focus on three-dimensional medical image segmentation and analysis. Students will gain hands-on research experience working with medical imaging data, computational methods, and reproducible AI workflows under mentorship through the iCARE Lab.

</div>

<div class="news-entry" markdown="1">

### June 2026 — Mammography Research Accepted for SEET 2026

Our paper, **“A Reproducible Multi-View Mammogram Classification Baseline Using Dual-View Fusion on VinDr-Mammo,”** was accepted for presentation and publication in the proceedings of the **2026 International Conference on Software Engineering of Emerging Technologies (SEET 2026)**.

The work develops a reproducible deep-learning baseline for mammography classification using complementary mammographic views and investigates the value of dual-view information for breast cancer classification.

</div>

<div class="news-entry" markdown="1">

### May 2026 — Undergraduate Research Project Accepted to KSU Sophomore Scholars Program

My project, **“Beyond the Baseline: A Comparative Analysis of Vision-Language Models and ResNet50 for Few-Shot and Full-Data Cancer Classification,”** was accepted for the **2026–2027 Kennesaw State University Sophomore Scholars Program**.

The project will provide undergraduate students with a year-long mentored research experience investigating vision-language models and conventional deep learning approaches for medical image classification. Students will participate in research, scholarly dissemination, and presentation of their work through KSU's undergraduate research program.

</div>

<div class="news-entry" markdown="1">

### May 2026 — AortaSeg24 Challenge Paper Accepted in Medical Image Analysis

Our collaborative manuscript, **“Multi-Class Segmentation of Aortic Branches and Zones in Computed Tomography Angiography: The AortaSeg24 Challenge,”** was accepted for publication in *Medical Image Analysis*, pending minor revisions.

The study presents the AortaSeg24 Challenge and evaluates methods for multi-class segmentation of the aorta, its branches, and anatomical zones in three-dimensional computed tomography angiography (CTA) images.

</div>

### April 2026 — First-Year Scholar Presents Cancer Classification Research at NCUR 2026

Undergraduate researcher **Alayna Zafar**, a member of the 2025–2026 Kennesaw State University First-Year Scholars research team, presented the project **“Beyond the Baseline: A Comparative Analysis of Vision-Language Models and ResNet50 for Few-Shot and Full-Data Cancer Classification”** at the **National Conference on Undergraduate Research (NCUR 2026)**, held April 13–15, 2026, in Richmond, Virginia.

The project was conducted by **Alex Rojas, Sagar Patil, and Alayna Zafar** under mentorship through the iCARE Lab. The research compared vision-language models and ResNet50 for cancer image classification under few-shot and full-data learning settings. The work was also presented at the **KSU Symposium of Student Scholars**.

### Fall 2025 — iCARE Lab Established at Kennesaw State University

The **Intelligent Computing for Advanced Research in Healthcare (iCARE) Lab** was established in the School of Data Science and Analytics at Kennesaw State University.
