    <h2>About Me</h2>

    <div class="team-about__grid">

      <div class="team-about__text">
        <p>
          I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science and Analytics at Kennesaw State
          University and Director of the Intelligent Computing for Advanced Research in
          Healthcare (iCARE) Lab. My research focuses on artificial intelligence for
          healthcare, with particular interests in medical image analysis, deep learning,
          multimodal AI, medical image segmentation, image registration, and the development
          of reliable computational methods for clinical research.
        </p>

        <p>
          My research brings together academic, clinical, and industry collaboration. I
          collaborate with researchers at Emory University School of Medicine and the Atlanta
          VA Health Care System on AI and medical imaging research, and with Equifax on
          applied artificial intelligence, agentic systems, and data-driven research.
          Through the iCARE Lab, I mentor graduate and undergraduate students and work toward
          translating advances in AI and data science into practical solutions for healthcare
          and other real-world applications.
        </p>
